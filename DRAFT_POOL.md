## Position Flexibility

Position eligibility has three tiers:

- **Natural:** Full role toolkit and strongest rating. This is the figure's primary position.
- **Secondary:** Fully viable, but exchanges one signature ability for a role-specific ability.
- **Emergency:** Playable during drafts, injuries, or mid-match audibles, with a meaningful role penalty.

Simulation ratings are calculated separately for each eligible role. They should not apply a generic out-of-position penalty to one universal overall rating. For example, court vision makes LeBron James an elite General, but his Cannon rating should be based specifically on projection, passing, and ranged threat.

Versatility has its own draft value. A flexible player may rate slightly below a pure specialist while allowing stronger formations, safer drafting, and position changes during emergency audibles.

- **Michael Vick:** Cannon is primary; Runner is a high-level secondary role.
- **Magic Johnson:** Cannon is primary for roster accounting, with General and Runner as secondary roles.
- **Nikola Jokic:** Cannon is primary, with elite General and Bruiser secondary roles.
- **Lionel Messi:** Cannon is primary for roster accounting, with elite Runner and General secondary roles.
- **LeBron James:** Runner is primary for roster accounting; General, Bruiser, and Cannon remain high-level secondary roles.
- **Michael Jordan:** Runner is primary, Cannon is an elite secondary role, and General is an emergency role.
- **Serena Williams:** Runner is primary, with elite Cannon and strong General and Bruiser secondary roles.
- **Rob Gronkowski:** Bruiser is primary, with Runner as a high-level secondary role.
- **The Notorious B.I.G.:** Musical Communicator is primary, with Bruiser as a strong secondary role built around presence, composure, and pressure control.

## Curation Rules

- Track licensing and likeness requirements for living and recently deceased figures before confirming the commercial roster.
- Store only one card for each figure, even when that figure is eligible for multiple positions.
- Count each figure only under their primary position when measuring pool targets.
- Build each position from multiple disciplines so no role maps to only one profession or era.
- Balance fame with globally diverse discoveries and specialist picks.
- Treat ratings as role effectiveness, not a judgment of historical importance.
- Treat living public figures as licensed marquee content unless legal review approves another approach.
- Research names, cultural representation, estates, likeness rights, and disputed biographies before production.
