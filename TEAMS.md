## Conference Rules

- Each conference contains 15 teams.
- The Americas Conference contains teams from North and South America.
- The Eurasia-Africa Conference contains 13 Eurasian teams and two African teams: Cairo and Lagos.
- Every home field must remain geometrically symmetrical for competitive fairness.
- Field identity may alter layout, cover, sightlines, elevation, and travel distance, but biome effects remain small.
